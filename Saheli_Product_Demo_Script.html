💡 Best Practices for Recording Your Demo Video

  • Screen Zoom: Keep your browser at 100% zoom and resolution at 1920×1080 (Full HD).
  • Pacing: Do not rush. Let each screen action pause for 1–2 seconds so judges can clearly absorb the UI features.
  • Camouflage Demo: Make sure to practice the triple-click on the Saheli logo once before recording to trigger it smoothly.
  • Audio Quality: Use a quiet room and headphones with a mic (or OBS Studio noise suppression) for crisp audio.
